Interview Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before starting your interview.

Enable Camera

Your webcam will be used for facial expression, eye contact and posture analysis.

Enable Microphone

Your voice will be analyzed for clarity, confidence and communication skills.

Answer Within Time

Each question has a limited time for answering.

Professional Behaviour

Maintain eye contact, speak clearly and avoid unnecessary distractions.

Stable Internet

Ensure a good internet connection for a smooth interview experience.
